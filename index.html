About Me

I am a product developer and industrial designer working on defence soft goods, technical textiles, and mission critical equipment. My work covers the full path from concept and CAD through prototyping, environmental testing, and production.

Before defence, I developed architectural acoustic and lighting products, supported automotive production at Magna, and consulted with startups on tactical and modular equipment. I use AI tools throughout my process for research, visualization, and documentation, and I am expanding into apparel development through pattern making and digital patterns.
