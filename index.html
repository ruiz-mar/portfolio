About Me

I'm a Product Developer / Industrial Designer working across defence soft goods, technical textiles, and mission-critical equipment. I take products from early concepts and sketches through iterative design, CAD development, digital and physical prototyping, validation, testing, advanced materials and coatings, supplier collaboration, and production. I work across the full product lifecycle, from the first design direction through manufacturing, delivery, and into the hands of the customer.

My work bridges design, engineering, and manufacturing, combining tools such as SolidWorks, Rhino, CLO 3D, Lectra, and Macola with AI platforms including Krea and Claude. I take ownership of the projects I lead, driving development forward with accountability, initiative, and cross-functional leadership. I'm constantly looking for smarter ways to design, engineer, and manufacture products, with a strong focus on innovation, manufacturability, sustainability, and circular design.
